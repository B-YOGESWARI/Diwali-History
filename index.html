01

Fourteen Years Away

Rama, Sita and Lakshmana are traditionally described as spending fourteen years in exile, away from Ayodhya.

02

Forests and Trials

Their journey is remembered through the Ramayana as a time filled with challenges, encounters and courage.

03

The Journey Home

After exile and the victory over Ravana, they are traditionally believed to have returned towards Ayodhya.

04

Ayodhya Awaits

People of Ayodhya welcomed Rama by lighting rows of oil lamps throughout the city.

05

Light Over Darkness

The lamp became one of the most recognisable symbols of Diwali — light, hope and renewal.

And the story became a tradition. But Diwali is more than one story.

🪔

Lighting Diyas

Oil lamps fill homes, entrances and prayer spaces with warm light.

🌸

Rangoli

Patterns of powders and flowers decorate entrances and courtyards.

🙏

Prayer & Lakshmi Puja

Families seek blessings, prosperity and well-being for the year ahead.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family & Food

Gifts, sweets and shared meals become the memories we keep.

Light, togetherness and renewal remain common themes across regions.

🌫️

Smoke & Air

Fireworks can raise fine particles; monitoring studies record festival-period spikes.

❤️

Health & Noise

Smoke and loud sounds can be hard on children, elders, animals and sensitive lungs.

♻️

What Remains

Burnt paper and cracker waste can linger long after the celebration ends.
